## Eating for your terrain

These are general suggestions for your terrain phase.

They are not a treatment plan.

Your doctor may have given you a diet, a fluid limit or a medicine that depends on food. If so, keep to that first.

Do not cut back on food, protein or fluids for a child, or if you are older and frail, underweight or losing weight without trying.

If you have diabetes or are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask Dr. Glen in your portal chat before fasting or dieting. The same applies if you take any medicine or have ever had an eating disorder.

### Terrain Revive (Phase 1)

This phase is about rebuilding energy, so the aim is to build.

Eat nourishing, moist food that is easy to digest.

Include good fats and protein at most meals.

Cooked grains suit this phase.

Drink plenty of fluids.

This is not a time to fast.

### Terrain Repair (Phase 2)

This phase is about active repair, so the aim is to lighten the load.

Favor light food and broths.

Go easy on fat and heavy meat.

Check the cautions above before eating little or nothing.

If you have a fever, consider eating little or nothing while it lasts, and drink plenty of fluids.

Return to light food as it passes.

A fever in a baby needs prompt attention, as does a high or lasting fever. Confusion, trouble breathing or signs of dehydration with a fever need prompt attention too.

### Terrain Renew (Phase 3)

This phase is about restoring tissue, so the aim is to build gently.

Keep sugar low.

Eat modest amounts of whole-food starch.

Fill your plate with fresh greens.

Favor steamed and lightly cooked vegetables over fried food.

Keep protein moderate.

### Terrain Refresh (Phase 4)

This phase is about clearing what has built up, so the aim is to reduce.

Better out than in: help your body's own elimination with enough water, fiber and daily movement.

Eat lighter, simpler meals.

Choose warm, cooked food.

Check the cautions above before an overnight fast.

An overnight fast of 12 to 14 hours suits those with strength to spare.

### Terrain Relief (Phase 5)

This phase is about restoring balance, so the aim is steadiness.

Eat regular meals at regular times.

Include some protein or fat when you eat starchy food, and avoid long gaps between meals.

Eat more of your food earlier in the day.

When the seasons change, change your diet gradually.
