<p>Each phase of terrain has a season, a healing direction and a way of eating that supports it.</p>
<p>The seasons and directions are Dr Glen's own scheme.</p>
<p>The food columns show where the classical traditions agree, and where Dr Glen's scheme parts from them.</p>

<h2>A note on yin and yang</h2>
<p>This course uses yin and yang as the Huangdi Neijing defines them.</p>
<p>Macrobiotics reverses several pairs, such as heaven and earth.</p>
<p>Read macrobiotic food lists with that reversal in mind.</p>
<h2>What the evidence shows</h2>
<p>Human biology does cycle with the seasons.</p>
<p>About 23% of blood genes varied by season in one study (Dopico et al. 2015).</p>
<p>The Hadza gut microbiome changes through the year (Smits et al. 2017).</p>
<p>Eating earlier in the day improved metabolic markers in controlled trials.</p>
<p>Two small trials have tested Chinese constitution diets, with mixed results (Shen et al. 2010, Chung et al. 2023).</p>
<p>No trial has yet tested a diet matched to terrain.</p>
