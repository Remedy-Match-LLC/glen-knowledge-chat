Getting your remedies outside the US

Our store ships to addresses in the United States only.

At checkout, the address you enter must be a US address.

You can still receive your remedies almost anywhere in the world.

There are three ways to do it.

1. Use a package forwarder

A package forwarder gives you your own US address.

You enter that address at checkout, and we ship your order there as we would to any US customer.

The forwarder then sends it on to you, and handles the customs paperwork for your country.

Most forwarders let you combine several orders into one parcel, which lowers the postage.

You pay the forwarder directly for their handling and the international shipping.

Here are three forwarders we know of:

Stackry suits North America, including Canada and Mexico.

It can pre-pay duties, so nothing is owed at your door.

https://stackry.com

Shipito is simple to start with and ships to over 220 countries.

https://shipito.com

forward2me is well suited to the UK, Europe and Japan.

https://forward2me.com

Each one has a calculator on its website, so you can see the cost to your country before you sign up.

2. Send it to a friend or relative in the US

If you have family or a friend in the United States, you can enter their address at checkout.

They can send the parcel on to you, or keep it until you next see them.

3. Have a visitor bring it home

If you, a friend or a relative is visiting the United States, we can ship your order to where they are staying.

They can then carry your remedies home in their luggage.
